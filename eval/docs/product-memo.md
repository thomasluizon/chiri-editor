# Why we should drop the free tier

Our free tier was meant to bring in teams who would later pay. It mostly brings in individuals who never do: in the last year, 3% of free workspaces upgraded, and most of those already had a paid seat elsewhere in their company.

Running it is not free. Free workspaces generate 41% of support tickets and a fifth of our hosting bill.

Mistakes were made in how the tier was positioned, and the decision to keep it was taken without looking at these numbers. Removing it will definately improve things a lot.

I propose we replace it with a 30-day trial of the full product, starting next quarter. Existing free workspaces keep working for six months, with a clear prompt to upgrade or export.
