# Billing migration: week 6 update

This week the billing team continued working through the migration plan. We held our regular syncs with finance and support, reviewed open tickets, and updated the runbook with a few clarifications from last week's dry run.

Support volume was steady. Most tickets were about invoice formatting, which the new templates already address, and response times stayed inside our target.

The big news: every customer is now on the new billing system, two weeks ahead of schedule, and the old system will be switched off on March 3. Customers will recieve their first invoice from the new system on March 1.

Thanks to Priya and the finance team for the late nights on reconciliation. The numbers matched to the cent on the first full run.
