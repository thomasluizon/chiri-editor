# Your first week

Your laptop arrives on your first morning. Sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email, then change it when prompted.

## Requesting access

When it comes to getting access to the tools that you will need in order to do your job, there are a number of different systems that you will need access to, and each of these systems has its own process for requesting access, which can sometimes be confusing, especially for people who are new and have not yet had the chance to learn how things work here. In general, most of the access you need will be requested on your behalf by your manager before you start, but in some cases you may find that you still need to request access yourself, and if that happens, the best thing to do is to request it through the IT portal, which is the place where access requests are handled. It is worth keeping in mind that access requests can take some time to be processed, so it is a good idea to request access as early as you can.

The access form should be submitted by the new hire before the end of the first week, and it will then be reviewed by the IT team.

## Getting help

Post in #it-help with a screenshot and what you were trying to do. Someone from IT replies within an hour during working hours.
